               * Food Tests
               * Reducing Sugar Test/Benedict’s Test
               * Add 2 cm3 of Benedict’s solution with the 2 cm3 of the sample solution
               * Put the test tube in a hot water bath for 3 minutes at 80 C
               * Observe the colour change
               * Negative Result - Solution Remains Blue
               * Positive Result - Solution turns from Blue to Green, Yellow, Orange or Brick Red
               * Green - Low concentration of Reducing Sugar
               * Yellow/Orange- Moderate concentration of Reducing Sugar
               * Brick Red - High concentration of Reducing Sugar

                  * Non-Reducing Sugar Test
                  * Sample must first give negative result with Benedict’s solution
                  * Boil 2cm3 of the sample solution with 2cm3 of HCl in a hot water bath
                  * Cool the test tube and neutralise with 2 cm3 of NaHCO3 until it stops fizzing
                  * Add 1 cm3 of Benedict’s solution with the 2 cm3 of sample solution
                  * Put the test tube in a hot water bath for 3 minutes at 80 C
                  * Observe the colour change
                  * Negative Result - Solution Remains Blue
                  * Positive Result - Solution turns from blue to Green, Yellow, Orange or Brick Red
                  * Green - Low concentration of Non Reducing Sugar
                  * Yellow/Orange- Moderate concentration of Non Reducing Sugar
                  * Brick Red - High concentration of Non Reducing Sugar

                     * Fat Test/Ethanol Emulsion Test
                     * Add 2 cm3 of ethanol with the 2 cm3 of sample solution
                     * Mix it gently and wait 1 minute to allow the solid to settle
                     * Ethanol is then decanted into another test tube
                     * Add 2 cm3 of water into the test tube and shake properly
                     * Observe the colour change
                     * Negative Result - No Change
                     * Positive Result - Cloudy white emulsion forms




                        * Protein Test With Biuret Solution
                        * Add 2cm3 of Biuret Solution with the 2 cm3 of sample solution and mix properly
                        * Observe the colour change
                        * Negative Result - Solution Remains Blue
                        * Positive Result - Solution turns from Blue to Purple

                           * Protein Test Without Biuret Solution
                           * Add 2cm3 of NaOH with the 2 cm3 of sample solution and mix properly
                           * Add Cu(II)SO4 solution drop by drop
                           * Observe the colour change
                           * Negative Result - Solution Remains Blue
                           * Positive Result - Solution turns from Blue to Purple

                              * Vitamin C Test/DCPIP Test
                              * Add DCPIP to sample solution drop by drop
                              * Observe the colour change
                              * Negative Result - Solution remains dark blue
                              * Positive Result - Solution turns from dark blue to colourless

                                 * Starch Test/Iodine Test for plants
                                 * Dip the leaf in boiling water to kill it
                                 * Boil the leaf in hot ethanol to remove the chlorophyll
                                 * Use water to rinse and soften the leaf
                                 * Perform starch test using iodine solution which will turn blue black

                                    * Starch Test/Iodine Test General
                                    * Add a few drops of iodine solution with the 2 cm3 of sample solution
                                    * Observe the colour change
                                    * Negative result - Solution remains brown
                                    * Positive result - Solution turns from brown to blue-black

                                             * Cells Definition
                                             * Cells are the structural and functional unit of life
                                             * They are often incapable of independent existence

                                             * Cell membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus are common to all cells

                                                * Cell Membrane Definition
                                                * It is a partially permeable membrane
                                                * It controls the movement of substances in and out of cells
                                                * It surrounds the cell

                                                * It separates the cell from the outer environment
                                                * It allows osmosis and active transport to occur

                                                   * Cytoplasm
                                                   * It fills up the cell
                                                   * It holds the cell organelles within their place
                                                   * It is the place where metabolic reactions occur

                                                      * Nucleus
                                                      * It contains genetic information/hereditary information/DNA
                                                      * It controls cellular activity

                                                         * How do nuclei control cellular activity

                                                            * How does mRNA help in protein synthesis?

                                                               * Mitochondria

                                                                  * Ribosome
                                                                  * It is the place where protein synthesis occurs
                                                                  * With the help of mRNA

                                                                     * Cellulose Cell Wall
                                                                     * Cell wall is a rigid structure made of cellulose present outside the cell membrane of a plant cell
                                                                     * It helps to protect the cell and maintain the shape of the cell

                                                                     * It is completely permeable

                                                                        * Difference between cell wall and cell membrane
                                                                        * The cell wall is rigid but cell Membrane is not
                                                                        * The cell membrane has control but cell wall has no control
                                                                        * The cell wall is made of cellulose but cell membrane is made up of protein and lipids/phospholipid

                                                                           * Vacuole helps to maintain the shape of the cell/turgidity of the cell

Characteristics of Living Organisms
	Nutrition
	Taking in of materials for energy, growth and development
	Excretion
	Removal from organisms of toxic materials and substances in excess of requirements
	Reproduction
	The processes that make more of the same kind of organism
	Growth
	A permanent increase in size
	Movement
	An action by an organism causing a change of position or place
	Respiration
	The chemical reactions in cells that break down nutrient molecules and release energy
	Sensitivity
	The ability to detect and respond to changes in the environment

                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Definitions
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Syllabus
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Understand diffusion as the net movement of molecules or ions from a region of their higher concentration to a region of their lower concentration (i.e. down a concentration gradient), as a result of their random movement
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Understand osmosis as the net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential to a region of lower water potential, through a partially permeable membrane
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Understand active transport as the movement of molecules or ions into or out of a cell through the  cell membrane, from a region of their lower concentration to a region of their higher concentration  (i.e. against a concentration gradient), using energy released during respiration

Adaptation of plants
	Parts of plant
	Adaptation
	Most leaves have a large surface area and are thin
	Increases surface area for sunlight absorption and gas diffusion
	Stomata and guard cells
	Guard cells open and close the stomata which allow gases to diffuse in and out
	Spongy and palisade mesophyll cells
	Perform photosynthesis to produce glucose
	Air Spaces
	Carbon dioxide and oxygen move easily between cells. It also increases surface area for gas exchange
	Vascular bundle
	Deliver water and minerals to every part of the leaf and take away the glucose produced by photosynthesis
	Distribution of chloroplast
	Most of the chloroplasts are found in the palisade layer so they can get the most light and maximise rate of photosynthesis
	Upper epidermis
	Has a waxy cuticle to reduce water loss by evaporation
	Lower epidermis
	The lower surface of the leaf is full of little holes called stomata to allow gases to diffuse in and out.
